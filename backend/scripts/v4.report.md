# Script v4: call analysis report

Source: 60 outbound GHL calls of 60 seconds or more (35 contacts). Analyzed by Claude by reading the transcripts directly (no OpenRouter).

## What the calls were

| Group | Calls | Note |
|---|---|---|
| No usable transcript | 11 | GHL returned "No transcript found" or under 400 characters |
| Voicemail / call screening | 27 | Rep left a message or hit a screening service. Nothing to learn about live handling. |
| Live conversations | 22 | One more was a voicemail greeting, so 21 real conversations |

Outcome labels (`call-outcome:` tags) do not exist yet, so every call is "unknown". The results below are read from the conversations, not from booking data.

## The 21 live conversations

| What happened | Count |
|---|---|
| Next step secured (time, callback, contact or invite) | 10 |
| Soft yes (asked to resend email) | 1 |
| Declined | 5 |
| Not a clinic / wrong person / consultant / retired | 5 |

Of the cold calls (not a callback to an inbound lead), about half secured a next step.

## What worked

1. **A specific, evidence-based hook.** One rep cited the clinic's 191 five-star reviews and a website review that was emailed. The prospect said "I definitely am interested". This is the case for the AI `hook` and `opener` slots using real enrichment data.
2. **A low-pressure capacity question.** "If I could put 10 new cash-pay HRT and weight-loss patients in front of you next month, do you have room?" got "yes" and the prospect's cell number.
3. **Being upfront.** "This is a cold call, you can hang up, give me 30 seconds" got the 30 seconds and a callback time.
4. **Asking for a small step.** "Would you be opposed to a quick 15-minute conversation with a strategist?" worked when the prospect was not rushed.
5. **Locking the time and the contact method on the call**, sending the invite while still on the phone, and offering two specific slots.
6. **Sending price questions to the strategist.** Deflecting "what do you charge?" kept the call going.

## Where calls were lost

1. **Quoting a price early.** One rep named a monthly minimum on a cold call. The prospect ended it ("we're not in that position"). This is the single clearest loss. The script already forbids prices, and the new "How much does it cost?" response gives the deflection.
2. **Asking for monthly revenue early.** It was asked up front in 3 calls and answered in none (one prospect said no because a patient was in front of them). Keep revenue last in discovery, as the script already does.
3. **Unclear company name.** Prospects asked "who are you with?" or "what company?" in 6 calls, and the name was heard as "Well Lung Clinic" and "Wellness Click". Say the full name, then one sentence about what the company does.
4. **Wrong person.** 5 of 21 conversations were with consultants, a retiree, a promoter, or an old business. A quick screening question and a polite exit saves time.
5. **Bad audio.** At least two calls had "can't hear you" or breakup. Not a script problem, but worth checking the SDRs' headsets and connections.
6. **Prospect in a meeting or with a patient** (7 calls). Most turned into a callback when the rep asked for a specific time and best contact.

## What changed in v4

New response options (prospect says this, rep says that):
- Opener: Busy / with a patient
- Opener: Who is this? What company?
- Opener: How much does it cost? (no price; defers to the strategist)
- Opener: Didn't see your email
- Opener: Already have an agency (seen in only 1 call, so low confidence)
- Qualification: Owner not available (gatekeeper)
- Qualification: Not a clinic / wrong person

New FAQ items: "I never contacted you", "Who do you work with?", "Do you have a referral or affiliate program?"

`flows`: 15 branching conversation flows (ticking a response opens the rep's next question and the prospect's possible answers, which open further questions). **What is evidence and what is authored:** the objections, the wording that worked and the wording that failed come from the calls. The specific answer choices (for example "Under 10 / 10 to 30 / More than 30" new patients, or "waitlist / turn them away") are authored as sensible options, because only a few calls got that deep. Treat them as a draft for WCM sales to edit. Three example transcripts are in `research/transcripts/`.

No question, field key, outcome or recap logic was changed.

## Please confirm with WCM before relying on it

- The referral/affiliate answer comes from a rep saying "yes, we do" on one call. Confirm the program exists.
- The "short review of your website" email wording assumes the website-audit email is sent before calls.
- Reps quoted prices and a 12-month commitment in the one long call (about 20 minutes). Nothing from that was put into the script. Decide the official pricing stance with sales.

## Limits

49 usable calls from 31 contacts is a small sample. Treat these as patterns to test, not proven results. Re-run after more calls and after outcome tags exist.

Rollback: delete `scripts/v4.json`, and the server serves the previous script again.
